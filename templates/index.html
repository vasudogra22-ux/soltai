Agent Connect

Our AI Agent handles complex queries first — using full business context to resolve issues instantly. Only when it can't, a ticket is automatically created and a real human takes over. Nothing gets missed, customers never wait.

How It Works
1
Customer Sends a Query
A visitor asks something complex — custom pricing, partnerships, or a tricky issue the chatbot can't fully handle.
High-level queries
2
AI Agent Attempts Resolution
Our AI Agent steps in with full business context — checking policies, pricing rules, and past conversations to resolve it intelligently.
AI-first response
3
Resolved, or Auto-Ticket Created
If the AI Agent resolves it — done. If not, a support ticket is generated automatically within seconds. Nothing falls through the cracks.
Instant auto-escalation
4
Human Agent Takes Over
Your team gets notified with full context and conversation history — picks up exactly where the AI left off, no repeated questions for the customer.
